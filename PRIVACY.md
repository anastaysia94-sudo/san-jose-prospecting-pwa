# Privacy and responsible use

This app is local-first and does not transmit the prospect list, email drafts, status data, or activity logs to an application server.

- Send one reviewed message at a time.
- Contact only the publicly listed business inbox associated with that lead.
- Record and honor every opt-out or do-not-contact request.
- Do not invent client results, testimonials, portfolio claims, or false urgency.
- Export a backup before switching devices or clearing browser data.

Email clients and ChatGPT open only after an explicit user action. Their own privacy policies apply.
